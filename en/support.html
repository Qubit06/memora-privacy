Frequently asked questions

Why am I not getting reminders?

Check Memora’s notification permission and “Alarms & reminders” access in Android settings. Battery saving, background restrictions, and Do Not Disturb settings can also affect notifications. Setting names vary by device.

How do I enable notifications?

Open Android Settings → Apps → Memora → Notifications and enable notifications. Depending on your device, exact alarm access may be under Special app access → Alarms & reminders.

Where are reminders stored?

Reminders are stored in a SQLite database on your device using Drift. Memora has no cloud synchronization. System backup behavior may depend on Android settings and the app’s configuration.

Does the app work offline?

Creating and storing local reminders does not require internet access. Voice typing may require internet access depending on the provider. Purchases, purchase verification, and restoration may require communication with Google Play. Sending a support email also requires a connection.

How do I request support?

Tap the email link. Include the issue, device model, Android and Memora versions, and steps to reproduce it. If helpful, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden.

How do I purchase Memora Unlimited?

The free version allows up to 10 active reminders. Memora Unlimited permanently unlocks unlimited active reminders with a one-time purchase; there is no subscription or monthly/yearly charge. Purchase in the app through the Google Play payment screen. Check the applicable price and Google Play account before paying. A Google Play connection may be required.

I paid, but Unlimited is not active. What should I do?

Check that the purchase is completed in Google Play and that you are using the correct Google Play account. The feature may not unlock until a pending payment completes. Check your internet connection and reopen the app. If the issue persists, email support with your app version and the error message; do not send card details or banking passwords.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the Google Play account that made the purchase and check your internet connection. Purchase entitlement is queried through Google Play. Steps for restoration after reinstalling or on another device, including whether it is automatic, depend on the actual Billing integration. Email us if you have a purchase or restoration problem.

Where can I get help with payments, cancellations, or refunds?

Memora Unlimited is not a subscription and has no recurring charge. For payments, purchase cancellations, and refunds, review Google Play’s applicable refund policies. Refund eligibility depends on Google Play’s terms. Email support for app issues or guidance.
