Privacy Policy

Privacy, made clear.

Learn how local data, permissions, and purchase information are handled in the first Google Play release of Memora.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

01Scope and product features

This policy is prepared for the first Google Play release of the Memora Android app offering Memora Unlimited through Google Play Billing. The Google Play developer name is QuBit. Memora is a local reminder app with no account or sign-in system, cloud synchronization, AI, or OCR features. The app contains no advertising SDK.

02Reminders and local storage

The reminders you create are stored using Drift in a local SQLite database in Android’s app data area. They may remain locally until you delete them or clear the app’s data. Memora has no cloud synchronization for these reminders. Android system backup and device transfer behavior may depend on device settings and the app’s backup configuration. Local storage does not mean system backups are disabled.

03Permissions and notifications

Notification permission is used to display reminder notifications. Exact alarm access is used to trigger reminders at their scheduled time. Microphone permission is requested only when you start voice typing. You can manage or revoke permissions in Android settings; doing so may affect the corresponding feature.

04Voice typing and third parties

During voice typing, the Android speech recognition provider may process audio data. Processing may take place on the device or on the provider’s servers, and an internet connection may be required. The provider’s own privacy terms apply. Providers and settings may vary by device. This policy does not guarantee how the provider stores or shares audio data.

05Deleting your data

You can delete reminders in the app. To remove local app data, clear Memora’s data in the storage section of Android settings; this deletes local reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the device. If data exists in system backups or is restored through device transfer, you may need to manage it separately in Android or your backup provider’s settings.

06In-App Purchases and Payments

The free version allows up to 10 active reminders. Memora Unlimited (product ID: memora_unlimited) permanently unlocks unlimited active reminders with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription or monthly/yearly charge. Prices may vary by country, currency, and Google Play pricing; the applicable price is shown on the purchase screen.

Payments are processed through Google Play’s payment infrastructure. Credit card numbers, card security codes, and banking passwords are not directly received or stored by Memora. Google Play may process purchase and payment information under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play terms.

The app may process product IDs, purchase status, and necessary purchase verification information through Google Play Billing to verify purchase status, unlock Unlimited, and restore purchases. As the Flutter Billing integration is not yet complete, the actual data fields, storage behavior, and any transfers to verification servers must be checked before publication and matched to this policy. This policy makes no definitive claim that data is not sent to the app’s own or third-party verification servers.

You do not need a Memora account. Purchase entitlement is checked through the relevant Google Play account. Restoration after reinstalling or on another device depends on querying purchase status with the same Google Play account and the actual Billing implementation; automatic restoration on another device is not guaranteed. Purchasing, verification, or restoration may require communication with Google Play and an internet connection.

Google Play’s applicable payment terms and refund policies may apply to refunds, cancellations, and payment disputes. Deleting local reminders, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete Google Play purchase history or payment records.

07Support and contact information

If you email support, your email address and the information you share in your message are used to respond to your request. The email service may process the message under its own terms. Please avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

08Children’s privacy

Memora has no membership or registration system. If you have a concern about children’s privacy or information accidentally included in a support message, contact us at the email address below.

09Policy changes

This policy will be updated when features or data processing practices change. The current text and its last updated date are published on this page.
